HQminiMap – High-res map & live data

=== DESCRIPTION (Steam BBCode, paste as is) ===
[h1]HQminiMap – your whole world, in high resolution, live[/h1]

[b]Scan your map. Choose your filter. Make a decision.[/b]
HQminiMap turns your Transport Fever 3 map into one sharp top-down [b]orthophoto[/b] and shows your network on top of it, live: towns, lines, stations, vehicles, industries and warehouses.

🌐 [b]English · Français · Deutsch · Italiano · Español · 简体中文[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]⚠ This mod works with the free HQminiMap app[/h2]
The mod does the in-game part (map export, screenshots, live data). The map itself is built and shown by the [b]HQminiMap desktop app[/b] (Windows, Linux, macOS), free and open source:
[url=https://github.com/Spit8/HQminiMap/releases/latest]➜ Download the HQminiMap app[/url]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]One-click map scan[/b] – pick a mode (Simple, x4, x16 or x64 images); the app sets the grid, camera and lighting for your map and your screen resolution, then starts the scan in game.
[*][b]High-resolution orthophoto[/b] – zoom down to the pixel, rotate, full screen, adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and gamma.
[*][b]Relief[/b] – colour the map by altitude, draw contour lines, or both.
[*][b]Clean basemap[/b] – black background, blue water and map outline to make your lines stand out.
[*][b]Live data[/b] – towns with their outlines, lines on their real tracks in the game's colours, stations and stops, moving vehicles with their load, industries and warehouses with their stocks.
[*][b]Filters[/b] – by name, transport mode, traffic, stock, line; save and share your filter setups.
[*][b]Control the game from the map[/b] – double-click to move the camera, or to open the in-game window of a vehicle, line, stop, factory or warehouse.
[*][b]Export the view[/b] as a PNG (×1, ×2, ×4) with an optional legend.
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to this mod and enable [b]HQminiMap[/b] in the mod list of your save.
[*][b]Install the HQminiMap app[/b] from the [url=https://github.com/Spit8/HQminiMap/releases/latest]download page[/url].
[*]Start the app: it finds the game's data folder by itself (otherwise, choose it in Options).
[/olist]

[h2]How to use it[/h2]
[olist]
[*]In game, click the [b]HQminiMap[/b] button in the bottom bar, then [b]Export map[/b].
[*]In the app ([b]GridLab[/b]), choose a capture mode.
[*]Click [b]Scan the map in game[/b]: the scan runs in your game, no need to open the mod window.
[*]The app builds the orthophoto by itself and opens it in the [b]Viewer[/b]. Enjoy!
[/olist]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Script mod:[/b] it only reads your game and takes screenshots; it does not change your world.
[*][b]No server, no account:[/b] the mod and the app talk through files in the game's data folder.
[*]The live data refresh rates can be tuned in the app ([b]Options → Live data[/b]) to keep big maps smooth.
[*][b]After a mod update, reload your save[/b]: the game keeps the old mod code until then.
[*]A new line may show provisional parts until the game has computed its whole track.
[/list]

[h2]More to come[/h2]
Ideas for the next versions: smartphone and tablet support, original game icons, a diagram generator, drawing on the map, richer (cross-)filtering.
